About This Site

This website was created as a task for COS30045 Data Visualisation at Swinburne University of Technology Sarawak. It explore the energy consumption of television avavilable on the Autralian market.

Data Sources

The data comes from the Australian Government's public Data catalog. It was cleaned and transformed in KNIME and only kept the usefull information to answer the questions in Task 1.
