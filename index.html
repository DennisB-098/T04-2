Welcome to the Appliance Energy Consumption website

The Hidden Cost

Australian households are buying larger, more powerful TVs

but most shoppers don't know how much electricity they actually use. Energy labels exist, but the data is buried in dense government files that are hard to interpret. Without clear comparisons, it's easy to pick a TV based on size or brand and ignore the ongoing running cost.

The Purpose

This website aims to provide clear, accessible information about the energy consumption of household appliances, helping consumers make informed decisions.
